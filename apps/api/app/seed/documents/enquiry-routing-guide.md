# Enquiry Routing Guide

Acme Professional Services receives client enquiries through the website form, the shared inbox and the client portal.
Every enquiry must be acknowledged within four working hours.

## Categories and owning teams

Billing and invoice questions are routed to the Finance team.
Requests for new engagements, scoping or proposals are routed to the Client Partnerships team.
Questions about tax filings, deadlines or HMRC correspondence are routed to the Tax Advisory team.
Payroll queries, including payslips and P60 forms, are routed to the Payroll Services team.
Access problems with the client portal or password resets are routed to the IT Service Desk.
Complaints are always escalated to the Client Experience Lead within one working day.

## Priority rules

Enquiries mentioning a statutory deadline within ten days are marked urgent.
Enquiries from clients on the Premier plan are prioritised over Standard plan enquiries.
If the category is unclear, the enquiry is assigned to the Client Services triage queue for manual review.
