# Expenses and Travel Policy

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims above 500 pounds require approval from a director before reimbursement.
Receipts are mandatory for every claim over 25 pounds.
Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run.

## Travel

Standard class rail travel is required for journeys under three hours.
Economy class travel is required for flights under six hours.
Hotel bookings must be made through the approved travel portal and must not exceed 180 pounds per night in London.
