# Client Onboarding Procedure

New clients must complete identity verification before any work begins, in line with anti-money-laundering rules.
The engagement letter must be signed by the client and countersigned by a partner.

## Steps

The Client Partnerships team creates the client record in the CRM within two working days of the signed engagement letter.
A conflict-of-interest check is completed by the Risk team before the client record is activated.
The client is invited to the client portal and assigned a named relationship manager.
A kick-off call is scheduled within ten working days of activation.

## Documents required

Clients must provide proof of identity, proof of address and, for companies, the certificate of incorporation.
Copies of identity documents are retained for five years after the relationship ends.
